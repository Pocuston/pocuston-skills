# Socratic Reading

A skill that checks and deepens your understanding of something you've already read: a book, article, paper, web page, documentation or transcript. Upload the file, paste the text or share a link, and the model asks you questions instead of summarizing for you.

## How it works

- **Grounded in the source:** questions come from the text you shared, not from what the model knows about it elsewhere.
- **No spoilers:** it asks how far you've read and never goes past that point.
- **Flexible units:** a chapter or section, a part of a long chapter, or the whole thing. The default mix opens each session with a quick question or two on earlier chapters.
- **Real tutoring:** one question at a time, step-by-step hints when you're stuck, honest corrections, and it stops once you've got it.
- **Picks up where you left off:** each session ends with a short *Reading status* block. Paste it into a new chat along with the source to continue.

## Example prompts

- "I've finished chapter 4 of this book, quiz me on it."
- "Here's an article I read yesterday: <link>. Check whether I really got the argument."
- "I finished the book. Let's do a whole-book session."
